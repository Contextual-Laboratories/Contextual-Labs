# Known Issues

A short, hand-maintained list of bugs and limitations that are already on
our radar — worth a check before filing a new issue, in case it saves you
a duplicate.

This list is curated by hand, not generated from GitHub Issues
automatically: an issue being open doesn't mean it's listed here yet, and
being listed here doesn't mean it's already fixed. For the live, complete
bug tracker, see [Issues](../../issues) (filter by `type: bug`).
